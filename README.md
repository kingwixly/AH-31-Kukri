The AH-31 Kukri is a 6.2-tonne twin-engine medium attack helicopter flown primarily by the Boscali Defense Force for national defense and close air support. It pairs an advanced nose-mounted sensor suite and small multimode radar with a 20mm rotary cannon fed from an 1,100-round magazine at 1,200 rounds per minute. It is an efficient, hard-hitting platform in terms of cost-to-performance, but relies on robust armor and sheer firepower rather than the stealth of designs like the Chicane.\
The Kukri is capable of carrying effective weaponry, light or heavy. The 20mm HEI chin-mounted cannon is a brilliant example of this compatibility, carrying 1,100 rounds that can be fired at a rate of approximately 1,200 rounds per minute. <UNAUTHORIZED MODIFICATIONS CREATED> In other words, it has a rapid democracy delivery rate, delivering 1,200 democracies per minute, or 20 democracies per second.\
The Kukri can also carry a chin-mounted 80 kW laser. It's effective at stopping nearby munitions and dealing damage to aircraft. It is limited by the power aboard the airframe and isn't as effective when firing at fast-flying or distant airframes.\
